RECIPE 01

Whipped White Chocolate Ganache

Whipping cream, gelatine mass and white chocolate.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity
Whipping Cream1,174 g
Gelatine Mass80 g
White Chocolate250 g

Total ingredient weight 1,504 g

Preparation

  1. Heat the whipping cream in a pot.
  2. Place the white chocolate in a separate bowl.
  3. Weigh the gelatine mass and set it aside.
  4. Once the cream starts to steam, add the gelatine mass and dissolve it over very low heat.
  5. Add the melted white chocolate and blend.

RECIPE 05

Strawberry Gel

Strawberry puree, pectin Napage, sugar and lemon juice.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity
Strawberry Puree1,000 g
Pectin Napage20 g
Sugar200 g
Lemon Juice20 g

Total ingredient weight 1,240 g

Preparation

  1. Heat the strawberry puree in a pot until all the frozen puree has melted and steam starts to appear.
  2. Mix the pectin into the sugar, then add to the hot puree. Cook for about 5–10 minutes, stirring often, until it gels. Test by placing a drop on a spoon: the drop should keep its dome shape.
  3. Stir in the lemon juice.

RECIPE 06

Chai Cream

Yemeni tea and cardamom-infused pastry cream with cinnamon and butter.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity
Milk1,600 g
Sugar320 g
Yemeni Tea40 g
Ground Cinnamon20 g
Cardamom Pods16 pcs
Ground Cardamom10 g
Condensed Milk150 g
Egg Yolk288 g
Cornflour144 g
Gelatine Mass160 g
Butter1,600 g

Preparation

  1. Mix the milk, sugar, Yemeni tea, ground cinnamon, cardamom pods and ground cardamom in a pot. Bring to the boil, then cover and let infuse for 10–15 minutes.
  2. Strain the milk mixture through a damp cheesecloth.
  3. Add more milk to bring the total weight back to 1,600 g. Add the condensed milk and stir to dissolve, to avoid burning.
  4. Make a pastry cream.
  5. Add the gelatine mass and stir to dissolve.
  6. Add the butter and blend with a hand blender until smooth.

RECIPE 07

Pastry Cream Base

Milk, sugar, egg yolk, cornflour, gelatine mass and butter.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity
Milk1,600 g
Sugar320 g
Egg Yolk288 g
Cornflour144 g
Gelatine Mass160 g
Butter1,600 g

Total ingredient weight 4,112 g

Preparation

  1. Heat the milk in a pot with 120 g of the sugar.
  2. Mix the egg yolk, cornflour and the remaining 200 g of sugar in a bowl.
  3. Weigh the gelatine mass and butter separately.
  4. When the milk boils, pour a little into the yolk mixture and mix thoroughly. Return the mixture to the hot milk and bring to the boil.
  5. Add the gelatine mass and stir slowly until it dissolves.
  6. Add the butter and blend.
